## What is CompEx?

CompEx is a data-driven exam preparation platform for the **GRE** and **GMAT**. Unlike traditional question banks with static, recycled problems, CompEx uses artificial intelligence to generate fresh, high-quality questions continuously — and gives you deep analytics to pinpoint exactly where you need to improve.

Think of it as the **LeetCode approach to exam prep**: habit-forming practice, transparent performance data, and a relentless focus on measurable improvement.

---

## What Makes CompEx Different

### AI-Powered Question Generation

CompEx doesn't rely on a fixed pool of questions. Our autonomous question generation pipeline — powered by **DeepSeek R1**, a state-of-the-art reasoning model — creates new problems around the clock across every topic and difficulty level.

Every question goes through a **4-phase chained pipeline**: metadata generation, question text creation, answer option construction, and step-by-step solution writing. This ensures each problem is internally consistent, properly calibrated, and comes with a detailed AI-generated explanation.

An automated quality control system continuously evaluates questions and removes any that fall below our quality threshold — replacing them with higher-quality alternatives. The result: your question bank is always growing, always improving, and never stale.

### 10+ Interactive Analytics Charts

CompEx doesn't just track whether you got a question right or wrong. It gives you a **comprehensive analytical dashboard** with over 10 interactive D3.js visualizations:

- **Activity Heatmap** — See your daily practice consistency at a glance, just like a GitHub contribution graph
- **Accuracy Trend Chart** — Track your accuracy and average speed over time
- **Topic Quadrant Analysis** — A 4-quadrant scatter plot that classifies every topic into Mastery (fast and accurate), Over-Invested (accurate but slow), Rushed (fast but error-prone), or Critical (slow and inaccurate)
- **Skill Radar Chart** — Multi-axis visualization of your strengths across all topics
- **Difficulty Distribution** — Understand what difficulty levels you're spending your time on
- **Section Distribution** — See how your effort splits across exam sections
- **Outcome Time Distribution** — Compare time spent on correct vs. incorrect answers against the community

### Automated Weakness Detection

You don't have to guess where to focus. CompEx's **Actionable Stats** engine automatically surfaces three critical insights:

- **Priority Areas** — Topics where your accuracy is below 65%, sorted by how often you attempt them. These are the areas hurting your score the most.
- **Speed Gains** — Topics where you're accurate (80%+) but slow (over 120 seconds per question). These are efficiency leaks where a small time improvement yields outsized score gains.
- **Strength Zones** — Topics where you're consistently at 90%+ accuracy across 5 or more attempts. Maintain these, but don't over-study them.

### Gamification That Drives Consistency

Exam prep is a marathon, not a sprint. CompEx keeps you engaged with:

- **Daily Streaks** — Track consecutive days of practice to build lasting study habits
- **XP System** — Earn experience points weighted by question difficulty. Level 1 questions earn 10 XP; Level 5 questions earn 500 XP. Every correct answer builds your profile.
- **Global Leaderboard** — See how you rank against other students preparing for the same exam. Your Global Rating blends practice XP (40%) and mock exam performance (60%) into a single score.
- **Collectible Badges** — Unlock achievements through challenges and milestones

### Full-Length Mock Exams with Post-Exam Analytics

Practice under real exam conditions with timed mock exams:

- **GRE Mocks**: 1 hour 28 minutes, section-timed with hard deadlines
- **GMAT Mocks**: 2 hours 15 minutes, matching the GMAT Focus Edition format

After each mock, your **Mock Dashboard** provides four dedicated charts:

- **Box Plot Chart** — Your score vs. the community score distribution (quartile visualization)
- **Scatter Plot** — Your scores across multiple mocks plotted against difficulty
- **Percentile Trend** — How your percentile rank has changed across mocks over time
- **Score Distribution** — Community histogram with your score marked

### AI Coach Solutions

Every single question comes with an **AI-generated step-by-step solution**. After you answer, the AI Coach breaks down the problem, walks through the reasoning, and explains why each answer choice is correct or incorrect. No more wondering "why was I wrong?" — every mistake becomes a learning opportunity.

## How CompEx Works

1. **Sign up** and choose your exam (GRE or GMAT)
2. **Explore** questions by topic, question type, or difficulty level
3. **Practice** at your own pace — the platform tracks every attempt, timing, and outcome
4. **Review** your analytics dashboard to see exactly where you stand
5. **Focus** on your Priority Areas and Speed Gains identified by the automated weakness detection
6. **Take mock exams** to simulate the real test and benchmark against the community
7. **Repeat** — your question bank keeps growing, and your analytics keep sharpening
