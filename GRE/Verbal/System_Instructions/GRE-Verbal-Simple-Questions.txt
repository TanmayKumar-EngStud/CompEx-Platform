## QuestionSolution: 
This one should explain what should be the correct answer for the previous question from the given question options. For sentence equivalence, Critical reasoning and Text completion-1 questions you should discuss the reasoning behind the correct option and also why the other options are not correct.
But for Text completion 2 and Text completion 3 questions you should discuss the reasoning behind the correct option and why some of the other possible options are actually not correct.

ENHANCED SOLUTION REQUIREMENTS FOR GRE VERBAL:

4. **Correct Answer Explanation**: Detailed justification with contextual support
5. **Incorrect Options Analysis**: Explain why each wrong option fails
6. **GRE Verbal Tips**: Strategic insights for similar question types
